### 0. Open questions first (these affect what you'll see later)

1. **Does the fire-whirl button appear in the disasters panel at all?**
   (Task 14 open question) `FireWhirlPanelButton`/`IntensityUnlock` both use
   `Object.FindObjectOfType<T>()`, and **Unity 5.6's `FindObjectOfType` does
   not find inactive GameObjects.** If the disasters panel starts inactive
   until you open that UI tab, the button (and the intensity-slider patch)
   may never install on a city where you never open that panel. **What to
   check:** open the disasters panel tab at least once after loading a city,
   then look for the "火災旋風 / Fire whirl" button. **If it's missing:**
   check `output_log.txt` for repeated `Diag` lines about the panel not being
   found — that confirms this is the cause, not a crash.
2. **Is `FeatureHost.framesPerMinute` (assumed `262144/1440`) correct?**
   (Task 9 note, Task 11 measurement task) **What to check:** set
   `MaxLifetime` to 2 in-game minutes in the mod's Options, spawn a whirl
   (manually via the panel button, once you've confirmed item 1 works), and
   time how long it actually survives against the in-game clock (not a
   real-world stopwatch — use the game's own minute counter). **If it's off:**
   only `FeatureHost.framesPerMinute` needs correcting — nothing else in the
   lifetime math depends on this constant being right.
